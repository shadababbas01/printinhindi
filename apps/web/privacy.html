What stays on your device

Registry document files (.docx) you upload, their parsed content, and everything rendered in the preview and print pipeline are processed entirely in your browser. These are not uploaded to, or stored on, our server.

What we do collect

To provide accounts, subscriptions and licensing, our server processes:

  • Account identity (email, via Supabase Auth)
  • Billing profile fields you provide (name, phone, business name, GSTIN if applicable)
  • Subscription/purchase/credit metadata
  • Device registration data (a random installation ID, device name, user agent)
  • Basic product-usage analytics events (see below) — never document content

What we never send to our server

  • The .docx file bytes you upload
  • Parsed document HTML/text/paragraphs
  • Registry party names, addresses, property details, signatures, or tables from your documents
  • Document filenames (excluded from analytics by default)

Payments

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. We do not store your card/UPI credentials — see Cashfree's own privacy policy for how they handle payment data.
